Buyer & Seller Paths

Buyer path

Customer account
  • Browse and search the marketplace
  • Save favourites to your wishlist
  • View product details and seller profile
  • Message the seller to negotiate price
  • Chat with text, images, voice notes, or call
  • Get notified when sellers reply

Seller path

Verified + subscribed
  • Register as Seller or upgrade from Customer
  • Submit ID documents for admin review
  • Wait for seller verification approval
  • Choose a subscription plan and pay via Paystack
  • Post items with photos, price, sizes & condition
  • Manage listings — edit, mark sold, or relist
